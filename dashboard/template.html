    How the numbers are made

    Built by tools/build_dashboard.py from data/history.csv, data/tracks.json and data/track-log.md. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

    Working sets count every set not typed warm-up. Most sets have no RPE, so this is an upper bound on hard sets (0-3 reps in reserve). Muscle credit uses Hevy's primary and secondary muscle groups (data/exercise-muscles.json). Targets per large muscle group follow guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md section 3: 2 days 4-6, 3 days 8-12, 4 days 10-14, 5 days 14-17, 6 days 15-20. Calves and arms run lower in a 3-day week.

    e1RM is Epley on sets of 1-12 reps, reps extended by 10 − RPE when an RPE is logged. A change of 1% or more counts as up or down. A comparison across more than four weeks off is marked as a restart. Judge strength over 3-4 weeks, not one session (guide/07-tracking-and-apps.md section 1.2).

    Tonnage is load × reps as logged: dumbbell loads are per dumbbell; bodyweight and assisted sets add none. Gap clock bands are from templates/progression-tracks.md section 8.

## 5. Calorie and macro plan

Maintenance estimate: about 2,850-3,000 kcal/day. RMR is about 1,830-1,850 kcal by Mifflin-St Jeor and Katch-McArdle, times an activity factor for 10k+ steps, light running and lifting. The 10+ lb gain suggests the pre-baseline intake was about 3,300+ kcal/day.

| | 3-day training week | 5-6-day training week |
|---|---|---|
| Calories/day | 2,500 | 2,700 |
| Protein | 180 g (about 2.2 g/kg) | 180 g |
| Fat | about 70 g | about 75 g |
| Carbohydrate | about 290 g | about 325 g |

- Average deficit about 300-400 kcal/day, inside the about 500 kcal/day limit in `guide/06-recovery-nutrition-cardio.md` section 4.
- Run days: add about 100 kcal per mile, mostly carbohydrate.
- Count alcohol in the totals.
- Creatine monohydrate 3-5 g/day.
- Protein in 4 feedings of about 45 g.
- Fat target and carbohydrate split are rules of thumb, not cited targets.

Tracking app: MyFitnessPal (signed up 2026-10-21). Set the goal manually rather than accepting the app's suggestion: 2,500 kcal as the default (3-day weeks), raised to 2,700 in 5-6-day weeks. Macro goals: protein about 29%, fat about 25%, carbohydrate about 46% of calories (180 g / 70 g / 290 g at 2,500). Do not let the app add back exercise calories from lifting or steps; the plan already counts them. Add back about 100 kcal per mile run.

## 6. Adjustment rules

Track all food and drink from day one. Weigh every morning; compare weekly averages. Expect a 1-3 lb rise in the first 2 weeks from water and glycogen; do not cut for it. Judge changes over 3-4 weeks.

| Signal over 3-4 weeks | Change |
|---|---|
| Losing 0.25-0.75 lb/week, lifts climbing | None |
| Weight flat and waist unchanged after 4 weeks | Cut about 150 kcal |
| Losing more than 1 lb/week, or lifts stalling | Add 150-200 kcal on 5-6-day weeks |

## 7. Expected timeline

| Period | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Weeks 0-2 | Scale may rise 1-3 lb (water, glycogen). Fast strength gains, mostly neural |
| Weeks 2-12 | Fastest phase. Lifts climb toward 2024 levels. Fat loss about 0.5-0.75 lb/week, partly hidden by muscle regain |
| Week 12 | About 5-9 lb less fat, 2-5 lb more lean mass. PBF about 13-14% at about 176-179 lb |
| Months 4-6 | Regain slows as previous muscle and strength return |
| After about 6 months | Recomposition becomes inefficient; choose a lean bulk or a short cut |

These are estimates from `research/body-recomposition.md` KQ2, not trial results.

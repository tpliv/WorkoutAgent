## If a day moves

- Any day falls through: the next session is still the next one in order (Full B, L3, U3, then L1); do not skip ahead.
- If Thursday's deadlift felt rough, drop Saturday's deadlift V sets (priority-stack drop, not a missed exposure).
- Do not run L3 and L1 on consecutive days (squat V, then squat H).

The lifter has a gym sauna (`guide/06-recovery-nutrition-cardio.md` §6.1). None until Mon 9 Nov (R+1), and only once fluids and food are back to normal.

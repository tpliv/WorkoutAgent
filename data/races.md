## Read (revised 2026-11-08 with the lifter's answers)

- **Faster by 44 s, mostly from pacing.** 2026 went out 23 s/mi faster over
  miles 1-5 and gave back 16 s/mi over miles 6-10. 2025 ran the first half
  conservatively and closed in 8:12 and 7:28, so it left time on the course.
- **The higher heart rate is mostly the extra 16 lb.** Average HR 175 vs 169
  (+6 bpm, +3.6%). The lifter weighed 165 lb in 2025 and about 181 lb now
  (+16 lb, about +10%). The energy cost of running a mile rises roughly in
  proportion to body mass (inference; same basis as the 100-120 kcal/mile
  add-back in `data/body-composition.md`). Carrying about 10% more mass a little
  faster, into a stronger wind, for 3.6% more heart rate means the absolute
  aerobic engine is probably better than in 2025, not worse. Per pound of
  bodyweight, fitness looks about the same. The first read ("no gain, maybe a
  small loss") is withdrawn. Temperature is ruled out: both mornings were
  40-46 °F. Wind (8-16 mph, gusts 22-23, against 5-8 mph) likely added some
  effort in exposed stretches; not quantified.
- **Mile 9 was a shoe-tie stop plus fatigue.** Garmin moving time is only 2 s
  under total time, so the stop sits inside the 9:01 lap. Without it, mile 9
  was likely near 8:30-8:40, in line with miles 7-8 and 10. The R-3 squat
  (155 x 5, Thu 5 Nov) is no longer a needed explanation. The race-week rule
  (last heavy lower at R-4) still stands.
- **Fatigue from mile 7 is real.** Miles 3-6 averaged about 8:26; miles 7-10
  (setting aside the stop) about 8:35-8:40. The first half was a little hot for
  current fitness.
- **Next year.** Double-knot the shoes. Even pacing at about 8:30-8:35 for miles
  1-8, then race the last 2: roughly 1:25:30-1:26 on today's fitness. If the
  recomposition brings bodyweight down while fitness holds, pace at the same
  heart rate should improve with it. Beyond that, the time has to come from more
  weekly running than the current under-10 miles, and from heavy lower-body
  lifting plus a little plyometric work, which improves running economy by about
  2-8% (`research/running-and-lifting.md` KQ3). Keep the last heavy squat at R-4
  or earlier.

### Phases and ratings (added 2026-11-06)

Rules: `templates/progression-tracks.md` sections 5 and 5.1-5.3; evidence in `research/retraining-after-layoff.md` and `research/effort-ratings-and-progression-without-rpe.md`.

- **Rate the top set, one tap:** 7 easy, 8 on target, 9 hard, 10 nothing left. If rep 4 is clearly easy, keep going, 6 at most, and log the reps. Extra reps now count: 6 @ 8, or 6 unrated reps, earns the big step.
- **Squat: regain phase** until its e1RM reaches 263 lb (about 220 x 4 @ 8, 90% of the 2024 best), the first top set at RPE 8.5 or harder, or 9 Jan 2027. +10 lb when the top set reads 7 or easier, never more than +10 a session. A gap of 8-14 days does not block the step.
- **Row: regain phase** until e1RM 128 lb (a track load of about 95). +10 lb when every set reaches 8 at a logged RPE 7 or easier, or the first set reaches 11.
- **Deadlift: caution** until 9 Jan 2027. +10 only with a logged RPE 7 or easier, never twice in a row; any low-back pain, hold.
- **Bench and press** are back near their 2024 level (95-100% and about 90%) and run the normal rules.
- **Rep-out:** after two unrated top sets of exactly 4 in a row, the next heavy session's last back-off is a rep-out (to about RPE 9, 10 reps at most). Deadlift and press are at one unrated 4 each.

Notes on each (written 2026-10-21 for the first pass):

- **Squat.** 135 will feel light against the 2024 peak (250 x 5). That is intended. If the top set comes in at RPE 7 or lower, section 5 allows +10 lb jumps until it stops; expect to reach 155-165 within three heavy exposures.
- **Bench.** The recent 95 x 8 sets were at an unknown RPE. 95 x 4 should be about RPE 6-7; take the +5 lb big step when it is (it does not wait for the step counter).
- **Deadlift.** Untrained for 18 months. On the first L2 (or Full B): warm up, then 95 x 4, 115 x 4, 135 x 4, 145 x 4, adding 10 lb per set until 4 reps feel like RPE 8. Stop there, log that load as the track load, then do 2 back-offs at 93%. The 2024 history (155 x 6) suggests the stopping point will be somewhere between 135 and 155, but let the RPE decide.
- **Overhead press.** 60 is about 80% of the recent estimate. With 2.5-lb plates as the smallest, the press adds 5 lb every second successful exposure; the step counter in `data/tracks.json` keeps the count between sessions.
- **Row.** The recent rows were 12s at 65. 70 for 4 x 6-8 at RPE 8 may be light; if all four sets hit 8 at RPE 7 or lower, go straight to 80 next time.
- **Pulldown.** Running as the pull-up track until a pulldown set at about bodyweight reaches 8 reps; then switch to the bar (section 2). You did bodyweight pull-ups for up to 8 in 2024, so this switch may come early.

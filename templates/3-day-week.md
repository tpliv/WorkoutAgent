# 3-day week

Last updated: 2026-10-21

**Use when** you can train three days this week. All three blocks are merged: Full A, Full B, Full C. Every main lift still gets its heavy and its volume exposure, so the progression tracks advance exactly as they do in a 6-day week; what shrinks is accessory volume (about 65 hard sets, 8-12 per large muscle group, roughly half the 6-day week). Rules: `guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`. Per-lift progression: `templates/progression-tracks.md`.

| Order | Session | Merges | Main-lift exposures | Hard sets | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full A (= L1 + U1) | Lower A + Upper A | Squat H, Bench H, Row H | 22 | 65-75 min |
| 2 | Full B (= L2 + U2) | Lower B + Upper B | Deadlift H, Press H, Pull-up H | 22 | 65-75 min |
| 3 | Full C (= L3 + U3) | Lower C + Upper C | Squat V, Bench V, Deadlift V, Press V, Row V, Pull-up V | 21 (+2 optional) | 65-75 min |

**Placement:** Mon Full A, Wed Full B, Fri Full C, or any three days with at least one rest day between sessions where possible. Consecutive days are acceptable in a pinch; avoid putting Full A and Full B back to back if you can, since both carry heavy top sets. **Cardio:** on the four non-lifting days, about 24 h from Full A and Full B; this is the week to bank most of the weekly minutes (`guide/06-recovery-nutrition-cardio.md`, section 5).

**Pointer:** Full A advances it L1 to L2, Full B to L3, Full C back to L1. If the pointer is not at L1 when the week starts, do the three merged pairs from where it stands (for example L3+U3, L1+U1, L2+U2); if it sits mid-block, pair across blocks (U1+L2, U2+L3, U3+L1), which is allowed because consecutive half-sessions are always one lower and one upper.

**Why this is not a deload:** top sets stay at RPE 8 and the tracks advance. Volume is about half of the 6-day week, which is above the one-third dose (9 lower-body sets a week, one session) that held muscle size and still added strength in young adults (Bickel 2011; older adults lost size on both reduced doses) and far above the one-session, one-set maintenance floor (Spiering 2021). Age flag: lifters in their late 40s and 50s should keep the optional sets in Full C and avoid two 3-day weeks in a row.

Key: **H** = heavy exposure (1 x 4 top set at RPE 8, then back-offs 7% lighter). **V** = volume exposure (sets at 90% of the lift's current top-set load, no top set). In merged sessions H exposures run 2 back-offs instead of 3 and V exposures run one set fewer. RPE 8 = 2 reps in reserve. Rest 2-3 min on compounds (3-4 min after a top set, 3-5 min for deadlift), 60-90 s on isolation; superset pairs: 60 s between exercises, 2 min after the pair. Warm-up ramp on the first compound only (bar x 5, 80% x 2-3, plus 60% x 3 between them once the top set is 225 lb (about 102 kg) or more, a coaching convention rather than evidence); one feeder set at 60-70% for each later main lift.

## Full A: squat heavy, bench heavy, row heavy

| # | Exercise | Sets x reps | Target RPE (RIR) | Rest | Dumbbell / machine substitution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back squat, full depth[^deep] (track: squat H) | 1 x 4 top set, then 2 x 4 at -7% | Top set 8 (2 RIR); back-offs 7-8; stop if a set reaches 9 | 3-4 min after the top set; 2-3 min between back-offs | Hack squat or leg press (hypertrophy only; keep the barbell squat at least once a week for strength) |
| 2 | Bench press (track: bench H) | 1 x 4 top set, then 2 x 4 at -7% | Top set 8 (judge by RPE, not by the planned load: a 4 @ 9.5 here is a "hold," not a miss); back-offs 7-8 | 3-4 min; 2-3 min | DB bench or machine press (hypertrophy only) |
| 3 | Barbell row (track: row H)[^lats] | 3 x 6-8 | 8 on every set | 2-3 min | Chest-supported DB row; machine row |
| 4 | Seated leg curl[^curl] | 3 x 10-15 | 8-9; last set may reach 10 | 60-90 s | Lying leg curl (less growth); Nordic curl |
| 5a | DB lateral raise, neutral rotation (thumbs level, not up)[^lat] | 4 x 12-20 | 8-9 | 60 s, then 5b | Cable or machine lateral raise |
| 5b | Overhead cable triceps extension[^tri] | 3 x 10-15 | 8-9 | 2 min after the pair | DB overhead extension; skull crusher with the arms angled back |
| 6 | Standing calf raise, full stretch, 1 s pause[^calf] | 3 x 8-12 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | Leg-press calf raise; single-leg DB calf raise |

22 sets. Dropped from the split form of L1 and U1: Romanian deadlift, leg press, incline DB press, face pull, core, one back-off per main lift, one row set. If over 75 minutes, cut in this order: calves to 2 sets, triceps to 2 sets, lateral raise to 3 sets (`guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`, section 4.3).

## Full B: deadlift heavy, press heavy, pull-up heavy

| # | Exercise | Sets x reps | Target RPE (RIR) | Rest | Dumbbell / machine substitution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deadlift (track: deadlift H) | 1 x 4 top set, then 2 x 4 at -7% | Top set 8; back-offs 7-8 | 3-5 min; 3 min | Trap-bar deadlift (hypertrophy; keep the conventional deadlift at least once a week for strength) |
| 2 | Overhead press (track: press H) | 1 x 4 top set, then 2 x 4 at -7% | Top set 8 (judge by RPE); back-offs 7-8 | 3 min; 2-3 min | Seated DB press; machine shoulder press; landmine press |
| 3 | Weighted pull-up, or lat pulldown if under 5 clean bodyweight reps (track: pull-up H)[^lats] | 3 x 5-8 | 8 on every set | 2-3 min | Lat pulldown; assisted pull-up machine |
| 4 | Leg press or Bulgarian split squat | 3 x 8-12 | 8 | 2 min | Hack squat; DB split squat; walking lunge; hip thrust for glute emphasis[^ht] |
| 5a | Reverse pec deck[^rear] | 3 x 15-20 | 8-9 | 60 s, then 5b | Bent-over DB rear fly; face pull |
| 5b | Cable lateral raise[^lat] | 2 x 12-20 | 8-9 | 60 s, then 5c | DB or machine lateral raise |
| 5c | Incline DB curl[^bi] | 3 x 10-12 | 8-9 | 2 min after the trio | Cable curl; EZ-bar curl; preacher curl |
| 6 | Standing calf raise[^calf] | 2 x 8-12 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | Leg-press calf raise |

22 sets. Dropped from L2 and U2: front squat, lunge, leg curl, flat DB press, core, one back-off for the press, one pull-up set, one lateral-raise set, two calf sets. If over 75 minutes: drop calves, then the lateral raise.

## Full C: every main lift at volume

| # | Exercise | Sets x reps | Target RPE (RIR) | Rest | Dumbbell / machine substitution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back squat (track: squat V) | 3 x 6 at 90% of the current squat top-set load | 7-8; no set past 9 | 2-3 min | Hack squat or leg press (hypertrophy only) |
| 2 | Bench press (track: bench V) | 3 x 6 at 90% of the current bench top-set load | 7-8 | 2-3 min | DB bench; machine press |
| 3 | Deadlift (track: deadlift V) | 2 x 4 at 90% of the current deadlift top-set load | 6-7; crisp, fast reps | 2-3 min | Trap-bar deadlift |
| 4 | Overhead press (track: press V) | 2 x 5 at 90% of the current press top-set load | 6-7 | 2 min | Seated DB press; machine press |
| 5 | Barbell row (track: row V)[^lats] | 3 x 8-10 at 90% of the row track load | 7-8 | 2 min | Chest-supported DB row; cable row |
| 6 | Pull-up or lat pulldown (track: pull-up V)[^lats] | 2 x 6-8 with the added load reduced (or bodyweight) so the sets land at RPE 7 | 7 | 2 min | Lat pulldown |
| 7 | Seated leg curl[^curl] | 3 x 10-15 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | Nordic curl; DB RDL |
| 8 | Incline DB press, 30 degrees[^incline] | 3 x 8-12 | 7-8 | 90 s to 2 min | Incline machine press; low-to-high cable fly |
| 9 (optional) | Standing calf raise[^calf] 2 x 10-15, **or** DB lateral raise[^lat] 2 x 12-20 | | 8-9 | 60 s | |

21 sets (+2). The lifts alternate lower and upper so that, when two stations are free, squat and bench (and deadlift and press) can be run as alternating paired sets with 90 s between them; this cuts about 10 minutes and still leaves each lift about 3.5 min between its own sets. Paired sets are for volume sets only; never pair a heavy top set (compound supersets cost some pulldown strength in one trial, Iversen 2024; same-muscle pairs lose volume, Zhang et al. 2025). Dropped from L3 and U3: leg extension, core, pushdown, curl, one set of each V exposure. Full C carries the incline press because Full A has no room for it; it is the week's one direct upper-chest exposure.

## Progression rules in brief

**Main lifts** (full rules: `templates/progression-tracks.md`): top set 4 reps at RPE 8. At RPE 8 or lower, the next H load rises 5 lb (squat, deadlift); bench and press rise 5 lb after every second such top set (step counter, `progression-tracks.md` section 5). RPE 8.5-9: hold. RPE 10 or fewer than 4 reps: hold and count a miss; two misses at one load is a stall, reset 7.5% and rebuild. The second and third main lifts in a merged session are judged by RPE: a planned load that comes in a full RPE heavy is a hold, not a miss. V sets are 90% of the current top-set load and never move the track. Row and pull-up: double progression, +5 lb when every set reaches 8; the 3-set merged form applies the rule to the sets done. Gaps: hold the load if a lift has gone 8-21 days untrained; reduce 5-10% beyond 21 days.

**Accessories:** double progression. Fixed load; add reps until every set reaches the top of the range at RPE 9 or less; then the smallest increment and back to the bottom. A 3-day week skips sets, never resets a load.

**Deload** (if this 3-day week is the scheduled deload week, or two readiness flags have persisted; `guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`, section 4.5): same three sessions; top sets at RPE 6-7 (about 90% of the track load); one back-off; V exposures 2 sets each; accessories 1-2 sets. A 3-day week is **not** a deload otherwise.

## Scaling

**Up to 4 days:** split block B. The week becomes Full A, L2, U2, Full C (`templates/4-day-week.md`). You gain front squat or hack squat, lunge, a second leg curl, two more calf sets, core, flat DB press, a full 4-set pull-up, a third lateral-raise set and a third back-off on the press: about 17 more hard sets.

**Up to 5 or 6 days:** split A as well (L1, U1, L2, U2, Full C), then C (all six). Split order is the reverse of the merge order: B first, then A, then C.

**Down to 2 days (emergency):** Full A and Full B with main lifts only (top set plus one back-off per H lift; row and pull-up 3 sets) plus one lower and one upper accessory each (leg curl and lateral raise in A; leg press and reverse pec deck in B). The pointer ends at L3; next week starts with Full C or L3 (`guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`, section 4.6).

**If a session is lost:** do not reshuffle. Next training day, do the owed merged session; the pointer carries into next week (`guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`, sections 4.1 and 4.4).

## Weekly volume tally (hard sets; indirect work at half credit)

| Muscle group | Direct sets | With indirect credit | Target (3-day week) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quads | 9 (squat 6, leg press 3) | 9 | 8-12 |
| Hamstrings | 6 (leg curl 6) | ~8.5 (deadlift 5 x 0.5) | 8-12 |
| Glutes | 3 (deadlift H) | ~8.5 (squat, leg press, deadlift V at 0.5) | 8-12 |
| Chest | 9 (bench 6, incline 3) | ~11.5 (press 5 x 0.5) | 8-12 |
| Back (lats, mid-back) | 11 (row 6, pull-up 5) | ~15 (deadlift, reverse pec deck at 0.5) | 8-12 |
| Side delts | 6 (lateral raise 6; 8 with the optional set) | ~8.5 (press 5 x 0.5) | 8-12 |
| Rear delts | 3 (reverse pec deck) | ~8.5 (rows and pull-ups 11 x 0.5) | 8-12 |
| Biceps | 3 (incline curl) | ~8.5 (rows and pull-ups 11 x 0.5) | 8-12 |
| Triceps | 3 (overhead extension) | ~10 (presses 14 x 0.5) | 8-12 |
| Calves | 5 (7 with the optional set) | 5-7 | 8-12 (small muscle at the low end; `guide/01-training-principles.md`, section 1) |

Every large muscle is trained in all three sessions; every small muscle gets at least one direct exposure plus indirect credit in two or three sessions, which satisfies the spine's "every muscle 2x." Per-session maximum for any muscle: 6 sets (chest in Full C), under the spine's 3-5-per-session guideline by at most one set. Total: about 65-67 hard sets, roughly 53% of the 6-day week.

## Footnotes on low-confidence choices

Confidence ratings follow `research/exercise-selection-and-session-design.md`, KQ2.

[^deep]: Full-depth squat: more glute and adductor growth than a half squat in 17 men (Kubo 2019); better transfer to all depths in 53 trained men (Pallares 2020). Moderate.
[^ht]: Hip thrust matched the squat for glute growth in untrained adults but not for quads or adductors; authors declared competing interests (Plotkin 2023). Moderate.
[^curl]: Seated over lying leg curl: one within-subject MRI trial, n = 20 (Maeo 2021). Moderate.
[^calf]: Standing over seated calf raise: 14 untrained adults (Kinoshita 2023); long-length emphasis from 42 young women (Kassiano 2023). Moderate, small samples.
[^incline]: Incline for upper chest: one trial, 47 untrained men training once a week for 8 weeks (Chaves et al. 2020, Int J Exerc Sci): incline-only grew upper-pec thickness more than flat-only and more than a flat + incline combination. Moderate, single trial.
[^lat]: Lateral raise for the side delt: EMG only, so Low. Coratella 2020 (IJERPH; 10 bodybuilders) found medial-delt activation highest with neutral humeral rotation on the way up (neutral > external) and internal rotation on the way down; its abstract's external-rotation conclusion conflicts with its own results, hence the neutral, thumbs-level cue. The overhead press activated the side delt almost as much as the raise (27.9% vs 30.3% MVIC; bench 5%; Campos 2020, J Hum Kinet), so the raise is here to add side-delt volume at low fatigue cost, not because pressing misses the muscle. Low.
[^tri]: Overhead triceps extension: one 12-week within-subject MRI trial, n = 21 (Maeo et al. 2023, Eur J Sport Sci; online 2022): overhead grew the long head more and the lateral and medial heads more too (+14.6% vs +10.5%). A direct overhead-versus-pushdown comparison in 9 novice women was not significant (Stasinaki 2018, J Funct Morphol Kinesiol). Moderate.
[^rear]: Reverse pec deck for the rear delt: EMG only (Barbell Medicine synthesis). Low.
[^bi]: Incline DB curl: women-only trials (n = 63 untrained, Kassiano 2025; n = 38 recreationally trained, no between-group differences, Zabaleta-Korta 2023). Moderate.
[^lats]: Pull-up, pulldown and row variants are interchangeable for the lats on EMG (Lehman 2004); no hypertrophy head-to-head yet. Low. Rows are kept for the mid-traps.

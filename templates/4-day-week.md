# 4-day week

Last updated: 2026-10-21

**Use when** you can train four days this week. Two blocks are merged (A and C) and one is split (B): Full A, L2, U2, Full C. Every main lift gets its heavy and its volume exposure, so the tracks advance exactly as in any other week; volume sits in the middle (about 82 hard sets, 10-14 per large muscle group). Rules: `guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`. Per-lift progression: `templates/progression-tracks.md`.

| Order | Session | Form | Main-lift exposures | Hard sets | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full A (= L1 + U1) | Merged | Squat H, Bench H, Row H | 22 | 65-75 min |
| 2 | L2 Lower B | Split | Deadlift H | 19 | 55-65 min |
| 3 | U2 Upper B | Split | Press H, Pull-up H | 20 | 55-65 min |
| 4 | Full C (= L3 + U3) | Merged | Squat V, Bench V, Deadlift V, Press V, Row V, Pull-up V | 21 (+2 optional) | 65-75 min |

**Why merge A and C and not B:** block C merges cheapest (all volume sets, no top sets). Of the two heavy blocks, keeping B split gives the deadlift its own day and the press a fresh slot (`guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`, section 1.3).

**Placement:** preferred Mon Full A, Wed L2, Thu U2, Sat Full C. Acceptable: Mon Full A, Tue L2, Thu U2, Fri Full C; the squat top set on Monday and the deadlift top set on Tuesday are different patterns and the RPE cap sets the load, and coaches run consecutive days on 4-day templates without issue (`research/flexible-frequency-programming.md`, KQ4). **Cardio:** rest days first; otherwise after U2 (the only pure upper day), 20-30 min.

**Pointer:** Full A advances it L1 to L2; L2 to U2; U2 to L3; Full C back to L1. If the pointer is not at L1, write out the next six half-sessions and merge two consecutive pairs, preferring L3+U3, then L1+U1, then L2+U2 (cross-block pairs allowed if the pointer sits mid-block).

Key: **H** = heavy exposure (1 x 4 top set at RPE 8, then back-offs 7% lighter). **V** = volume exposure (sets at 90% of the lift's current top-set load, no top set). Merged sessions run one back-off fewer and one V set fewer. RPE 8 = 2 reps in reserve. Rest 2-3 min on compounds (3-4 min after a top set; 3-5 min for deadlift), 60-90 s on isolation; superset pairs: 60 s between exercises, 2 min after the pair. Warm-up ramp on the first compound (bar x 5, 80% x 2-3, plus 60% x 3 between them once the top set is 225 lb (about 102 kg) or more, a coaching convention rather than evidence); one feeder set at 60-70% for later main lifts.

## Full A: squat heavy, bench heavy, row heavy

| # | Exercise | Sets x reps | Target RPE (RIR) | Rest | Dumbbell / machine substitution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back squat, full depth[^deep] (track: squat H) | 1 x 4 top set, then 2 x 4 at -7% | Top set 8 (2 RIR); back-offs 7-8; stop if a set reaches 9 | 3-4 min; 2-3 min | Hack squat or leg press (hypertrophy only; keep the barbell squat at least once a week for strength) |
| 2 | Bench press (track: bench H) | 1 x 4 top set, then 2 x 4 at -7% | Top set 8 (judge by RPE: a 4 @ 9.5 here is a "hold," not a miss); back-offs 7-8 | 3-4 min; 2-3 min | DB bench or machine press (hypertrophy only) |
| 3 | Barbell row (track: row H)[^lats] | 3 x 6-8 | 8 on every set | 2-3 min | Chest-supported DB row; machine row |
| 4 | Seated leg curl[^curl] | 3 x 10-15 | 8-9; last set may reach 10 | 60-90 s | Lying leg curl (less growth); Nordic curl |
| 5a | DB lateral raise, neutral rotation (thumbs level, not up)[^lat] | 4 x 12-20 | 8-9 | 60 s, then 5b | Cable or machine lateral raise |
| 5b | Overhead cable triceps extension[^tri] | 3 x 10-15 | 8-9 | 2 min after the pair | DB overhead extension; skull crusher with the arms angled back |
| 6 | Standing calf raise, full stretch, 1 s pause[^calf] | 3 x 8-12 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | Leg-press calf raise; single-leg DB calf raise |

22 sets. If over 75 minutes, cut in this order: calves to 2, triceps to 2, lateral raise to 3.

## L2: Lower B (deadlift heavy)

| # | Exercise | Sets x reps | Target RPE (RIR) | Rest | Dumbbell / machine substitution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deadlift (track: deadlift H) | 1 x 4 top set, then 2 x 4 at -7% | Top set 8; back-offs 7-8 | 3-5 min; 3 min | Trap-bar deadlift (hypertrophy; keep the conventional deadlift at least once a week for strength); block pull |
| 2 | Front squat or hack squat | 3 x 6-10 | 7-8 | 2-3 min | Leg press; goblet squat; safety-bar squat |
| 3 | Walking lunge or Bulgarian split squat | 3 x 8-12 per leg | 8 | 2 min | Step-up; single-leg leg press; hip thrust for glute emphasis[^ht] |
| 4 | Seated leg curl[^curl] | 3 x 10-15 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | Nordic curl; lying leg curl |
| 5 | Standing calf raise[^calf] | 4 x 8-12 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | Leg-press calf raise |
| 6 | 45-degree back extension or Pallof press | 3 x 10-15 | 8 | 60 s | Reverse hyper; bird dog; suitcase carry |

Superset 4 and 5. 19 sets.

## U2: Upper B (press heavy, pull-up heavy)

| # | Exercise | Sets x reps | Target RPE (RIR) | Rest | Dumbbell / machine substitution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Overhead press (track: press H) | 1 x 4 top set, then 3 x 4 at -7% | Top set 8; back-offs 7-8 | 3 min; 2-3 min | Seated DB press; machine shoulder press; landmine press |
| 2 | Weighted pull-up, or lat pulldown if under 5 clean bodyweight reps (track: pull-up H)[^lats] | 4 x 5-8 | 8 on every set | 2-3 min | Lat pulldown; assisted pull-up machine; neutral-grip pulldown |
| 3 | Flat DB press or dips | 3 x 8-12 | 7-8 | 2 min | Machine chest press; weighted push-up |
| 4 | Reverse pec deck[^rear] | 3 x 15-20 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | Bent-over DB rear fly; face pull |
| 5 | Cable lateral raise[^lat] | 3 x 12-20 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | DB or machine lateral raise |
| 6 | Incline DB curl[^bi] | 3 x 10-12 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | Cable curl; EZ-bar curl; preacher curl (distal bias) |

Superset 4 with 5, or 5 with 6. 20 sets.

## Full C: every main lift at volume

| # | Exercise | Sets x reps | Target RPE (RIR) | Rest | Dumbbell / machine substitution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back squat (track: squat V) | 3 x 6 at 90% of the current squat top-set load | 7-8; no set past 9 | 2-3 min | Hack squat or leg press (hypertrophy only) |
| 2 | Bench press (track: bench V) | 3 x 6 at 90% of the current bench top-set load | 7-8 | 2-3 min | DB bench; machine press |
| 3 | Deadlift (track: deadlift V) | 2 x 4 at 90% of the current deadlift top-set load | 6-7; crisp, fast reps | 2-3 min | Trap-bar deadlift |
| 4 | Overhead press (track: press V) | 2 x 5 at 90% of the current press top-set load | 6-7 | 2 min | Seated DB press; machine press |
| 5 | Barbell row (track: row V)[^lats] | 3 x 8-10 at 90% of the row track load | 7-8 | 2 min | Chest-supported DB row; cable row |
| 6 | Pull-up or lat pulldown (track: pull-up V)[^lats] | 2 x 6-8 with the added load reduced (or bodyweight) so the sets land at RPE 7 | 7 | 2 min | Lat pulldown |
| 7 | Seated leg curl[^curl] | 3 x 10-15 | 8-9 | 60-90 s | Nordic curl; DB RDL |
| 8 | Incline DB press, 30 degrees[^incline] | 3 x 8-12 | 7-8 | 90 s to 2 min | Incline machine press; low-to-high cable fly |
| 9 (optional, include in 4- and 5-day weeks when under 70 min) | Standing calf raise[^calf] 2 x 10-15, **or** DB lateral raise[^lat] 2 x 12-20 | | 8-9 | 60 s | |

21 sets (+2). Alternate squat and bench (and deadlift and press) as paired sets with 90 s between when two stations are free. Paired sets are for volume sets only; never pair a heavy top set (compound supersets cost some pulldown strength in one trial, Iversen 2024; same-muscle pairs lose volume, Zhang et al. 2025). Full C carries the incline press because Full A has no room for it.

## Progression rules in brief

**Main lifts** (full rules: `templates/progression-tracks.md`): top set 4 reps at RPE 8. At RPE 8 or lower, the next H load rises 2.5 kg (squat, deadlift) or 1 kg (bench, press). RPE 8.5-9: hold. RPE 10 or fewer than 4 reps: hold and count a miss; two misses at one load is a stall, reset 7.5% and rebuild. The bench top set in Full A, taken after the squat, is judged by RPE: a planned load that comes in a full RPE heavy is a hold, not a miss. V sets are 90% of the current top-set load and never move the track. Row and pull-up: double progression, +2.5 kg when every set reaches 8. Gaps: hold the load if a lift has gone 8-21 days untrained; reduce 5-10% beyond 21 days.

**Accessories:** double progression. Fixed load; add reps until every set reaches the top of the range at RPE 9 or less; then the smallest increment and back to the bottom. Short weeks skip sets, never reset loads.

**Deload** (scheduled week 5-7 of the block, or two readiness flags): same four sessions; top sets at RPE 6-7 (about 90% of the track load); one back-off; V exposures 2 sets; accessories 1-2 sets.

## Scaling

**Up to 5 days:** split block A. The week becomes L1, U1, L2, U2, Full C (`templates/5-day-week.md`). You gain the Romanian deadlift, leg press, core, a third back-off on squat and bench, a fourth row set, incline DB press in U1, and a face pull: about 19 more hard sets.

**Up to 6 days:** split C as well (`templates/6-day-week.md`): L3 and U3 restore the leg extension, core, pushdown, curl, and the full V set counts.

**Down to 3 days:** merge block B into Full B: Full A, Full B, Full C (`templates/3-day-week.md`). Full B keeps the deadlift, press and pull-up exposures (press at 2 back-offs, pull-up at 3 sets), the leg press, reverse pec deck, a 2-set lateral raise, the incline curl and 2 calf sets; it drops the front squat, lunge, leg curl, DB press and core.

**If a session is lost:** do not reshuffle. Next training day, do the owed half-session (or merge it with the next one); the pointer carries into next week (`guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`, section 4.4).

## Footnotes on low-confidence choices

Confidence ratings follow `research/exercise-selection-and-session-design.md`, KQ2.

[^deep]: Full-depth squat: more glute and adductor growth than a half squat in 17 men (Kubo 2019); better transfer to all depths in 53 trained men (Pallares 2020). Moderate.
[^ht]: Hip thrust matched the squat for glute growth in untrained adults but not for quads or adductors; authors declared competing interests (Plotkin 2023). Moderate.
[^curl]: Seated over lying leg curl: one within-subject MRI trial, n = 20 (Maeo 2021). Moderate.
[^calf]: Standing over seated calf raise: 14 untrained adults (Kinoshita 2023); long-length emphasis from 42 young women (Kassiano 2023). Moderate, small samples.
[^incline]: Incline for upper chest: one trial, 47 untrained men training once a week for 8 weeks (Chaves et al. 2020, Int J Exerc Sci): incline-only grew upper-pec thickness more than flat-only and more than a flat + incline combination. Moderate, single trial.
[^lat]: Lateral raise for the side delt: EMG only, so Low. Coratella 2020 (IJERPH; 10 bodybuilders) found medial-delt activation highest with neutral humeral rotation on the way up (neutral > external) and internal rotation on the way down; its abstract's external-rotation conclusion conflicts with its own results, hence the neutral, thumbs-level cue. The overhead press activated the side delt almost as much as the raise (27.9% vs 30.3% MVIC; bench 5%; Campos 2020, J Hum Kinet), so the raise is here to add side-delt volume at low fatigue cost, not because pressing misses the muscle. Low.
[^tri]: Overhead triceps extension: one 12-week within-subject MRI trial, n = 21 (Maeo et al. 2023, Eur J Sport Sci; online 2022): overhead grew the long head more and the lateral and medial heads more too (+14.6% vs +10.5%). A direct overhead-versus-pushdown comparison in 9 novice women was not significant (Stasinaki 2018, J Funct Morphol Kinesiol). Moderate.
[^rear]: Reverse pec deck and face pull for the rear delt: EMG only (Barbell Medicine synthesis; Sweeney thesis). Low.
[^bi]: Incline DB curl: women-only trials (n = 63 untrained, Kassiano 2025; n = 38 recreationally trained, no between-group differences, Zabaleta-Korta 2023). Moderate.
[^lats]: Pull-up, pulldown and row variants are interchangeable for the lats on EMG (Lehman 2004); no hypertrophy head-to-head yet. Low. Rows are kept for the mid-traps.

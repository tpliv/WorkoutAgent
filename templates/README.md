# Templates: how to use them

Last updated: 2026-10-21

The four weekly templates are **views of one rotation**, not four programs. Pick the one that matches the days you have this week, run it, and next week pick again. Loads and progression rules are identical in all four; only accessory volume and back-off sets differ. The reasoning and the full rule set are in `guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`; the per-lift progression rules are in `templates/progression-tracks.md`.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `3-day-week.md` | Full A, Full B, Full C (all three blocks merged). About 65 hard sets, 65-75 min per session. |
| `4-day-week.md` | Full A, L2, U2, Full C (blocks A and C merged). About 82 sets. |
| `5-day-week.md` | L1, U1, L2, U2, Full C (block C merged). About 101 sets. |
| `6-day-week.md` | L1, U1, L2, U2, L3, U3 (nothing merged). About 122 sets, 55-65 min per session. |
| `progression-tracks.md` | Starting loads, the heavy and volume exposures, increment, hold, miss, stall, reset, deload and gap rules, logging and e1RM. |

## The rotation in one table

| Slot | Name | Main-lift exposures | Accessories (split form) |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Lower A | Squat **H** | Romanian deadlift, leg press or split squat, seated leg curl, calves, core |
| U1 | Upper A | Bench **H**, Row **H** | Incline DB press, lateral raise, overhead triceps extension, face pull |
| L2 | Lower B | Deadlift **H** | Front or hack squat, lunge, seated leg curl, calves, back extension or Pallof press |
| U2 | Upper B | Press **H**, Pull-up **H** | Flat DB press or dips, reverse pec deck, lateral raise, incline DB curl |
| L3 | Lower C | Squat **V**, Deadlift **V** | Seated leg curl, leg extension, calves, core |
| U3 | Upper C | Bench **V**, Press **V**, Row **V**, Pull-up **V** | Lateral raise, pushdown, curl |

Order is fixed: L1, U1, L2, U2, L3, U3, then L1 again. Block A = L1 + U1, block B = L2 + U2, block C = L3 + U3. A block merged into one session is Full A, Full B or Full C.

**H** (heavy exposure): work up to one top set of 4 at RPE 8, then back-off sets 7% lighter (3 in a split session, 2 in a merged one; deadlift 2 in both). **V** (volume exposure): sets at 90% of the lift's current top-set load, no top set (one set fewer in a merged session). Each main lift has exactly one H and one V per pass of the rotation.

## How a week is built

1. Count the days you can train. **Merges = 6 minus days.** Merge block C first, then A, then B.
2. Open the matching template. Run the sessions in the order listed.
3. Every 3- to 6-day week completes one pass of the rotation, so the pointer (below) is back at L1 by the weekend and every main lift has had its heavy and its volume exposure.

| Days | Sessions | Merges |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | L1, U1, L2, U2, L3, U3 | 0 |
| 5 | L1, U1, L2, U2, Full C | 1 (C) |
| 4 | Full A, L2, U2, Full C | 2 (C, A) |
| 3 | Full A, Full B, Full C | 3 (C, A, B) |
| 2 (emergency) | Full A, Full B, main lifts only | pointer ends at L3 |

## The rotation pointer

The pointer is the next half-session you owe. Write it at the top of your log.

- A split session moves it forward one; a merged session moves it forward two.
- After any complete 3- to 6-day week it reads L1. It drifts only when you miss a session or run a 2-day week.
- **Never skip ahead.** The next time you train, do the half-session the pointer names, or merge it with the one after it. If a week ends mid-pass, next week starts from the pointer, not from L1: write out the next six half-sessions and apply this week's merge count to them (consecutive pairs only; prefer L3+U3, then L1+U1, then L2+U2; cross-block pairs such as U1+L2 are allowed when the pointer sits mid-block, since consecutive half-sessions are always one lower and one upper).
- Each main lift also carries a clock: days since its last H or V exposure. 0-7 days, normal; 8-21 days, hold the top-set load; over 21 days, reduce it 5-10%. Details: `progression-tracks.md`, section 8.

## Switching rules in brief

- **The day count changes week to week, never mid-block for the loads.** Top-set loads, rep targets and increments are the same in every template.
- **Shrinking a week** removes, in this order: core and extra calf sets; redundant accessories; second and third back-offs and fourth accessory sets; accessory compounds (leg press, incline press, lunge); then the direct work for undertrained muscles. The top set of each main lift is never removed, and each main lift's second (volume) exposure is kept down to 2 sets. Full list: `guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`, section 4.3.
- **Growing a week** reverses the merges: split B first (3 to 4 days), then A (4 to 5), then C (5 to 6). The templates' "Scaling" sections say exactly what comes back.
- **Nothing is postponed.** A set dropped in a 3-day week is gone; it returns when a longer week comes round. The rotation, not a make-up session, restores balance.
- **A short week is not a deload** unless it is the scheduled deload week (week 5-7 of the block), two readiness red flags have persisted for a week, or you are ill. A deload keeps the week's session count, puts top sets at RPE 6-7, cuts back-offs to one, V exposures to 2 sets and accessories to 1-2 sets.
- **Rack or bench taken:** do the next main lift first and come back; a substitute (leg press, DB row) never replaces a top set unless the station stays busy all session, and then it does not count for the track (`guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`, section 4.3, "Station taken").
- **Missed session:** do not reshuffle the week. Do the owed half-session next time you train and let the pointer carry.
- **Consecutive days are fine**; lower and upper alternate. Never two heavy lower half-sessions in one day; never more than two half-sessions in one day.

## Session conventions (all templates)

- RPE 8 = 2 reps in reserve; RPE 9 = 1; RPE 10 = none. Compounds live at RPE 7-8 (back-offs) and 8 (top sets); isolation at 8-9 with the last set allowed to reach failure.
- Rest: 3-4 min after a top set (3-5 min for deadlift), 2-3 min between compound sets, 60-90 s on isolation. Supersets: 60 s between the two exercises, 2 min after the pair.
- Warm-up: 3-5 min easy movement and mobility (optional; in a short session cut them before the 80% set), then on the first compound only: empty bar x 5, 80% x 2-3 of the day's top working load (the V load on a volume day). Once the top set is 225 lb (about 102 kg) or more, add 60% x 3 between them; that step is coaching convention, not evidence. Round to the nearest plate step, never below the bar. About 2-3 min. Later main lifts get one feeder set at 60-70%. Static stretches, if any, under 30 s.
- Substitutions: the right-hand column of every table gives dumbbell and machine alternatives. For hypertrophy, swap freely (free weights and machines grew muscle equally in the 5 studies that measured it; moderate confidence, Haugen 2023). For strength, keep the barbell squat, bench, deadlift and press themselves at least once a week; a substitute does not update the lift's track.
- Cardio: on rest days in 3- and 4-day weeks; after U1, U2 or U3 in 5- and 6-day weeks, 20-30 min, cycling or incline walking, never before a lower session.
- Logging: load, reps and RPE for every set; the track state for each main lift; bodyweight, sleep and a 1-5 readiness score per session. Format in `progression-tracks.md`, section 10.

## Flags

The research notes behind these templates were first compiled from abstracts and secondary summaries; each note now carries a 2026-10-21 primary-source verification addendum, and the templates follow its corrections. Figures still unverified are flagged in the templates' footnotes and in `guide/03-flexible-scheduling.md`. In particular: the per-session set ceiling (about 11 fractional sets) comes from a preprint that is not peer reviewed (Remmert et al.; figures confirmed by the FAU release and the authors' OSF data, and independently close to the ~11-set cap in Helms' Muscle and Strength Pyramid); the gap-modifier table is an inference from peer-reviewed detraining studies of complete cessation (mostly older), and the merge rules are inferences; no study has tested alternating 3- and 6-day weeks or merged versus separate sessions; the lateral raise, reverse pec deck and face pull rest on EMG or consensus only, and the reclined leg extension on a single trial in untrained men (Larsen et al. 2025).
